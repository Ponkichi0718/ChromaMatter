Qt 5.15.2 runtime notice
========================

ChromaMatter's Windows package carries Qt 5.15.2 shared libraries supplied by
the pinned PyMeshLab runtime.  This distribution uses Qt's LGPL-3.0-only
licensing option.  Qt also offers other licensing options; those alternatives
do not change the option used for this package.

The corresponding source is the exact Qt 5.15.2 source archive pinned by the
ChromaMatter corresponding-source manifest:

  https://download.qt.io/archive/qt/5.15/5.15.2/single/
  qt-everywhere-src-5.15.2.tar.xz
  SHA-256: 3a530d1b243b5dec00bc54937455471aaa3e56849d2593edb8ded07228202240

The full LGPL text used for the Qt component is provided in
LICENSE_QT_LGPL_3_0.txt.  Qt's ANGLE component has its own license text in
LICENSE_QT_ANGLE.txt.  RELINKING_EN.md and RELINKING_JA.md describe how to
replace the separately shipped Qt libraries in the PyInstaller one-folder
package.
