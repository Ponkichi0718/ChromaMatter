SQLite 3.50.4 public-domain status
==================================

All of the code and documentation in SQLite has been dedicated to the public
domain by the authors. Anyone is free to copy, modify, publish, use, compile,
sell, or distribute SQLite, in source code form or as a compiled binary, for
any purpose, commercial or non-commercial, and by any means.

SQLite source files carry this blessing:

    May you do good and not evil
    May you find forgiveness for yourself and forgive others
    May you share freely, never taking more than you give.

Official status and blessing:
https://www.sqlite.org/copyright.html
