# Sorca CM v1.1 — CutWorkflow

Apple Silicon / macOS 15 or later. Open `Sorca CM.app` after extracting the app ZIP.

This build includes the simplified cap and attached-peg controls, flat-centre cap regression repair, four-material plate counting repair, cut grounding and actual-cap orientation candidates, and retained-source Full Spectrum transfer for recut parts.

The bundle has ad-hoc signatures only. It is not Developer ID signed or notarized.
CI verifies native/helper command-line startup and signatures. Mac GUI operation, real-model slicing, printing and physical connector fit are not verified.

The Windows GUI and regression results do not constitute Mac testing. Do not overwrite your existing app until you have retained a copy.
This workflow uploads CI artifacts only; it does not publish an application release or change the website.

Source inventory and build evidence are in Contents/SharedSupport. Upstream and third-party notices remain included.
